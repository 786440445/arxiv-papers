**Link**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11620  
**PDF**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.11620.pdf

**Abstract (EN)**:
> We investigated the relationship among neural representations of vocalized, mimed, and imagined speech recorded using publicly available stereotactic EEG recordings. Most prior studies have focused on decoding vocalized speech, leaving other speech forms underexplored. Using representational similarity analysis (RSA), we find that imagined and mimed speech share significant representational similarity with vocalized speech in high-frequency bands (70-150 Hz), suggesting these covert forms preserve phonetic information. Furthermore, we demonstrate that a decoder trained on vocalized speech transfers to mimed speech with 78% accuracy and to imagined speech with 65% accuracy, highlighting potential applications in brain-computer interfaces for speech-impaired individuals.
